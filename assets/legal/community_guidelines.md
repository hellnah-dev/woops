# Community Guidelines

Woops is for adults looking for genuine connections. To keep it safe and friendly for everyone:

## Be real
- Use your own, recent photos where your face is clearly visible.
- Don't impersonate anyone or create fake accounts.
- Only adults are allowed: 18+, or older where your country's law requires it. If you think someone is underage, report them.

## Be respectful
- No harassment, threats, bullying, or unwanted sexual messages.
- No hate speech or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, religion, nationality, gender, sexual orientation, disability or any other characteristic.
- "No" means no. If someone isn't interested, move on.

## Keep it appropriate
- No nudity or sexually explicit photos.
- No violent, graphic or shocking content.
- No content involving minors, ever. We report it to the authorities.

## No scams or spam
- Never ask for money, gift cards, crypto or financial details.
- No advertising, promotions, or links to other services.
- Don't ask people to move to another app before you've built trust; scammers often do this.

## Stay safe
- Keep personal details (address, workplace, finances) private until you trust someone.
- Meet for the first time in a public place, and tell a friend where you're going.
- Use **Report** and **Block** whenever something feels wrong. Reports are confidential.

## What happens when rules are broken
Our moderation team reviews reports. Depending on how serious the issue is, we may remove content, warn, suspend or permanently ban an account, and contact law enforcement where necessary.
