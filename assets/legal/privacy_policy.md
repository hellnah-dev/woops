# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how [COMPANY NAME] ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects your information when you use Woops (the "App"). Woops is only for adults. You must be at least 18, or older where the law of your country requires it.

## 1. Information we collect

**You give us:**
- Account details: email address and password (passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider), or the basic profile from Apple or Google sign-in if you use it.
- Date of birth and country, used to confirm you meet the minimum age. Only your age is shown to others.
- Profile information: first name, gender, who you want to meet, what you are looking for, bio, prompts, interests, job title and city.
- Photos you upload. Before upload, photos are resized and their hidden metadata (EXIF, including GPS location) is removed on your device.
- Messages you send to your matches.
- Reports you make about other users, and verification selfies if you choose to get verified. Selfies are only visible to our moderation team.

**Collected automatically:**
- Approximate location, only if you allow it. We round it to about 1 km before storing it and never show it to other users. Others only see an approximate distance, which you can hide.
- Activity in the App: likes, passes, matches, blocks and the last time you were active.
- A push-notification token for your device, if you allow notifications.
- Error reports (for example, crash messages, app version and device platform) to help us fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 2. How we use it
- To create and run your account and show your profile to people who match your preferences.
- To create matches and deliver messages and notifications.
- To keep the community safe: age assurance, verification, investigating reports, preventing spam, fraud and abuse, and enforcing our Terms and Community Guidelines.
- To fix errors and improve the App.
- To comply with the law.

Legal bases (where GDPR/UK GDPR applies): performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in running a safe service, your consent (location, notifications, verification) and legal obligations. Some profile details you choose to share (for example, your intentions) may be sensitive in your country; you provide them voluntarily and can remove them at any time.

## 3. Who can see your information
- **Other users** see your public profile: first name, age, gender, photos, bio, prompts, interests, job, city, country, approximate distance (unless hidden) and verification badge. They never see your email, birthdate, exact location or reports.
- **Your matches** see the messages you send them and, if you allow it, when you have read their messages.
- **Service providers** who process data on our behalf: Supabase (database, authentication, storage and hosting) and Google Firebase (push notifications). They are bound by data-processing agreements.
- **Authorities**, when required by law or to protect someone's safety.

## 4. Blocking, reporting and safety
When you block someone, they can no longer see your profile or message you. When you report someone, your identity is never shown to the person you reported.

## 5. How long we keep it
We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), we permanently delete your profile, photos, likes, matches and messages. We may keep limited records of safety reports and moderation actions (without your profile) for up to [X] years to protect other users and meet legal obligations.

## 6. Your rights
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. You can edit or delete most information directly in the App. To make another request, contact us at the address below. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 7. International transfers
Our providers may store data in other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 8. Security
Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted by strict database security rules so that people can only access what they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password.

## 9. Children
Woops is not for anyone under 18 (or the higher minimum age in your country). If we learn that someone underage is using the App, we will remove their account. Please report suspected underage users in the App.

## 10. Changes
We will tell you in the App about important changes to this policy, and ask you to accept again where required.
